How it sounds

One path, from the file to your ears.

Every song takes the same route through the app. Each stage is yours to switch on, tune or leave alone.

  1. ♪Your musicFiles on your phone, your own Subsonic server, Tryptify Cloud or a stream.
  2. ≋Full resolution24- and 32-bit files are kept in floating point all the way through.
  3. ⫶DSP consoleBuses and an FX chain with 36 plugins, metered in LUFS.
  4. ◠Headphone EQAutoEQ for your model, then your own parametric EQ. It feeds the spectrum and visuals.
  5. ⇄Speed and pitchFaster or slower, by multiplier, semitones or BPM.
  6. ◉OutStraight to a USB DAC at the song's own sample rate, or through Android to Bluetooth and speakers.

Lyrics

Lyrics, lit by god rays.

Time-synced lyrics in glass letters, with light streaming from the line being sung. Pick one of nine looks, from Sunburst to Sea Glass, or aim the light yourself: follow the sung word, add dust, a beat flare or a slow orbit.

Discover

Your library, as a galaxy.

Discover is a map of genres you can fly through. Library galaxy puts every artist on your phone in the sky as a star beside their genre. Search it by any name: "dnb" finds drum and bass.

Mixer

A mixing desk, not a preset menu.

Build buses and FX chains from 36 plugins in six groups: EQ and filters, dynamics, distortion, modulation, space and utility. Every knob is real, and every chain can be saved.

Visuals

Watch what you hear.

A glass spectrum over the cover, a ridgeline that shows the last few seconds of the song, a waterfall, and 9,795 MilkDrop presets running live on projectM.

Speed and pitch

Any tempo, your pitch.

Change the speed by a multiplier, by semitones or straight to a BPM. Keep the pitch where it is, or let it follow the speed like a turntable.

Trypt Mania · beta

A rhythm game, on your own music.

Trypt Mania plays StepMania-style charts on the songs in your library. Bring packs or make your own in the map editor. It has its own player, so a round never touches your queue, scrobbles or status.

Liquid glass

Glass that bends the light.

The nav bar, mini player, search bars and panels bend what's behind their edges like real glass, tinted by whatever is playing. Over twenty glass looks, from Float to Holo.
