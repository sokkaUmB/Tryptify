# Tryptify 1.9.4

Your own music as a galaxy: every artist on your phone a star beside their genre, their albums planets and their tracks moons, and it works offline. Both galaxies get search, a stronger zoom and lighter controls. Lyrics find songs named by number and songs on compilations, and the player's panels read in light themes.

Each change is marked New, Changed or Fixed.

- New: **Your library as a galaxy.** Library galaxy, under Folders in Library, shows every artist on your phone as a star beside their genre on the genre map. Artists whose genre isn't recognised float above and below the disc.
- New: **Albums in orbit.** An artist's first album orbits nearest and each later one further out; albums you play a lot glow brighter. After eight albums the rest form an asteroid belt, and an album's extra tracks a ring. Tap a moon to play it, long-press a star to list every album and track.
- New: **Works offline.** Everything on it comes from the music and play history on your phone.

## Discover

- New: **Search the galaxy.** A search button on both galaxies finds a genre, by any of its names ("dnb" finds drum and bass), or an artist, and flies there.
- New: **One sky, two galaxies.** Your library galaxy sits in the genre galaxy's sky. Switch between them from the title bar and the view stays where it was.
- Changed: **Galaxy and Timeline are two pills.** The panel at the bottom of the map is two glass pills now. Surprise me is in the title bar, and how much you have explored is under the title.
- Changed: **A stronger zoom.** A pinch zooms further, starts at once, and stays on the planet you are zooming toward. Tapping a planet or a star brings it closer.

## Now playing

- Changed: **Glass Visualizer and Waveform buttons**, like Timer, Sound, Speed and Mixer above them.
- Fixed: **Panels read in light themes.** With dynamic colour in a light theme, the Speed panel, the audio tools and the preset browser had dark text on dark glass and lost their outlines. Their text is light and their pills and switches show.

## Lyrics

- Fixed: **Songs named by number.** Untagged songs named like "2-05 Song" or "CD1 - 05 - Song" are searched without the number, and no longer get "01" or "CD1" as their artist. Refresh the library once to fix songs already scanned.
- Fixed: **Compilations.** Songs on a Various Artists album are searched under their own artist.
- Fixed: **The right lyrics for your own files.** Songs on your phone are no longer looked up on TIDAL by a made-up number, which could bring back another song's lyrics.
