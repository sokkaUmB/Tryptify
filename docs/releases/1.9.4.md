# Tryptify 1.9.4

A release for USB DACs: exclusive mode now drives DACs that run in USB Audio 3.0's basic mode, Apple's USB-C headphone adapter among them, and every DAC plays at its full output, with the player's volume bar as the only volume.

Each change is marked New, Changed, Removed or Fixed.

## Exclusive USB DAC

- Changed: **More DACs in exclusive mode.** DACs in USB Audio 3.0's basic mode describe no formats of their own, and exclusive mode used to give up on them. Apple's USB-C to 3.5 mm adapter is one, because Android always puts it in that mode. They now play at 48 kHz, in 24-bit.
- Changed: **DACs at their full output.** The DAC's own volume is unmuted and set to full, never past 0 dB, whenever a song starts, as USB Audio Player Pro does. Android's volume never reaches a DAC in exclusive mode, so a DAC left turned down stayed down. The player's volume bar is the only volume now, and still starts at −24 dB. The DAC card shows the result, as HW vol 0 dB.
- Fixed: **DACs that refused every song.** Some DACs were turned away with "no alt setting matches" whatever played. Those whose packets are sized exactly to their rate, Apple's adapter included, play now. A song at a rate the DAC can't take, such as 44.1 kHz on a basic-mode DAC, says so and plays through Android instead.
- Changed: **The DAC card shows the DAC's release**, as rev, where it used to be labelled as the USB version.
