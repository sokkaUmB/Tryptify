# Tryptify 1.9.4

Trypt Mania, a StepMania-style rhythm game played on your own music: songs on your phone are charted for you, StepMania packs come in, and you can play in rooms with friends. Effects and mixer knobs can move with the music, the Theme Store shares a whole look, the app can be seen through an old TV, and every kind of text can have its own font, weight and width. Your own music becomes a galaxy too, lists are lighter Material 3, lyrics can be timed word by word on the server or by hand, and exclusive mode drives more USB DACs, Apple's USB-C headphone adapter among them, at their full output.

Each change is marked New, Changed, Removed or Fixed.

## Trypt Mania

A StepMania-style rhythm game played on your own music. Songs on your phone are charted for you, StepMania packs come in, and every run is judged and scored by StepMania's rules.

- New: **A rhythm game in your library.** Trypt Mania is in Library › Local, under Library galaxy. It opens on a start menu (Play, Multiplayer, Map editor, Packs and Settings, with the leaderboard in the top bar) over a demo chart that plays itself in your noteskin. The mini player never shows in it, and nothing Trypt Mania plays reaches your queue, scrobbles or Discord status.
- New: **The music wheel.** Play opens StepMania's music wheel: a folder per pack and one for your library, on an arc you turn by dragging, flinging or with the arrow keys. Sort by Pack, Mapped, Name, Duration or BPM from the top bar. Rest on a song and twelve seconds of it play from its drop, with the song's picture behind the wheel and a soft swish as each song turns into the middle.
- New: **A card for each song.** A glass card over the wheel shows the chart's Groove Radar, its tempo with a light on the beat, its level as stars cut through the glass and a graph of its notes per second. Glass pills under it pick the difficulty, Train or Play, and your best grade sits on the card. A song with no chart offers Generate chart.
- New: **Charts made from your songs.** Any MP3 or FLAC in your local library can be charted: Generate chart on the wheel, ⋮ › Generate a chart from a file, or Play in Trypt Mania from a song's menu anywhere in the app. The chart server splits the song into stems; the drums set the beat and the vocals, melody and bass place the arrows, in five difficulties from Beginner to Challenge. It runs in the background with its progress in a notification.
- New: **A chart server built in, Fast or Slow.** Charting works from the first launch, on the server listed under Settings › Connections as Built in. Trypt Mania › Settings › More › Chart processing picks Slow (a CPU server, minutes a song) or Fast (a GPU server you add, seconds a song). Chart a whole folder from its heading, or every song without a chart from ⋮.
- New: **StepMania packs.** Trypt Mania › Packs browses StepMania Online inside the app and imports a pack with one tap, in the background. You can also import a pack ZIP, or link a folder of unpacked songs, which is read where it is and copied nothing. Their songs join the wheel and get scores like any other, and a removed pack keeps your scores.
- New: **Your charts as a pack.** Every chart you make is saved to a folder you pick as the Tryptify pack, each song with its music and cover, ready for StepMania's or ITGmania's Songs folder. Save as ZIP makes one file that imports back like any pack.
- New: **Player options before every song.** StepMania's options, with the chart playing itself at the top so each one shows as you set it: speed type (XMod, CMod, MMod) and speed, music rate, Turn, Appearance, Reverse, Mini, Receptors, Noteskin, Judge (J1 to Justice) and Fail. One line sums up what is set, and everything but the rate is kept.
- New: **StepMania's rules and scoring.** Dance points, the percent to the hundredth, the seventeen grades from AAAA to D- plus F, the FULL COMBO banners and the life bar with all four fail types. A tap is judged at the moment your finger lands.
- New: **StepMania's retro arrows, in glass.** Three noteskins: Retro glass (the default), Retro (StepMania's own retro arrows) and Classic. Taps are coloured by beat and the pixel art is enlarged by whole pixels, so it stays sharp. Retro glass is made from your UI panel glass settings, so the notes change with the same sliders as the mini player.
- New: **StepMania's look, and god rays on every hit.** StepMania's judgements, grade art and typefaces, READY, GO! before the music, and receptors that flash on the beat. On Android 13 and later a hit streams god rays: white, then yellow, orange and red every fifty combo, and a turning rainbow from two hundred; a miss streams red. Settings › Graphics turns each effect on or off.
- New: **A dance stage behind the notes.** The lanes sit over an arcade dance pad, tilted away into a room made from the song's blurred cover, between two speakers that pump on the beat. The pad's panels glow while you hold a lane. Reduced motion keeps the stage still.
- New: **TV shaders.** Settings › Graphics › TV shader draws Scanlines, Diagonal, Triangular, Wave, CRT or CRT-Royale over the game, on Android 13 and later, on whole phone pixels so its lines don't shimmer. It has the same presets as the app's CRT effect.
- New: **Landscape.** Held sideways, the four lanes are a centred column, 40% of the width to start and anywhere from 25% to 100% in Settings › Graphics.
- New: **Results.** A ring of the run's judgements unrolls around your grade as the percent counts up, with a timing curve of how early or late you hit. When your recent runs lean one way, Centre my timing moves your audio offset for you. Play again, Songs and Practise your weakest section are underneath.
- New: **High scores and the leaderboard.** The ten best runs of each chart and difficulty are kept on your phone, under the name you set in Settings › More. The leaderboard shows every chart you've played through, one card per song. None of it leaves the phone.
- New: **Training Ground.** Train on the song card loops a section, slows it down with or without the pitch, and adds the metronome, Mirror, Shuffle or a ghost of your timing. Challenge gauntlets (Streams, Jumps, Holds, Alternating, Timing control) loop the parts of your chart that train one skill.
- New: **Map editor.** Pick a song and a difficulty, a new one included, and place Tap, Hold and Mine notes on a beat grid that snaps from 4ths to 32nds, with the song's waveform beside it and the song playing under it. Test plays the chart from just before the playhead and comes back, and saving rewrites only that chart.
- New: **Keyboard play and sync.** Settings › Controls gives each lane two keys, the arrows and D F J K to start with. Settings › Sync has an audio offset for phones and headphones that play late, read from your recent runs in one tap or found with Tap to sync, a pad you tap along to a 140 BPM click track.
- New: **Multiplayer rooms.** Multiplayer on the start menu plays as your Tryptify account. Join an open room or one by its code, or make your own, with a line of chat. The host picks a chart, everyone with the song on their phone plays it together after READY, GO!, scores show live and the results rank the room.

## Music links

- New: **Effects that move with the music.** Sliders on every tab of Player Visuals Studio, the CRT effect's too, can follow the kick, vocals, snare, hi-hats or a range you pick. Tap the ♪ beside a slider, or press and hold it, and choose Move with the music, then set how far, which way and how snappy; the rest, a formula included, is under More options. The editor fills the screen and closes only on Cancel, Done or Back. Your own setting never moves, so presets and the slider keep what you set.
- New: **Mixer knobs that move with the music.** Press and hold a smooth knob in the mixer's effects and choose Move with the music. It can listen to the song as it comes into the mixer, or to what reaches that knob's bus. You hear the link while you shape it, and Cancel puts the knob back. Links are saved with the mix and its presets.
- New: **Every link on one page.** Music links, from the ♪ at the top of Player Visuals Studio, lists every link by where it is (lyrics, player, UI panels, CRT effect, visualizer and mixer knobs), with pause, change and remove. One switch, Music links on, holds them all at your own values without removing any.

## Theme Store

- New: **Share your look.** Settings › Appearance › Theme Store › Share my look puts your colours, lyrics, glass, font, visualizers, maps and music links up as one theme, in the store or only by its code. The app draws the theme's picture as you share it. Nothing personal goes with it: a font you imported, your Bluetooth delay and your quality settings stay on your phone. Needs an account.
- New: **Try someone else's.** Two themes to a row under Popular, New and Mine, each with a picture of how it really looks, plus a search and Have a code? for one that isn't listed. Every theme previews live on the real player first, and Use this theme has a switch for each part, so you can keep your own font or colours. Your look before is saved for a one-tap undo.

## CRT effect

- New: **The whole app through an old TV.** Settings › Appearance › CRT effect, the last setting there and off until you turn it on, draws every screen as Scanlines, Diagonal, Triangular, Wave, CRT or CRT-Royale. Draw over puts it on the whole app or on the lyrics only. Android 13 and later, with the glass effects on.
- New: **CRT-Royale.** A consumer TV up close: beams that widen as they brighten, an aperture grille, slot or shadow mask, and light that halates, diffuses and blooms through the glass, each with its own slider. It is the heaviest of the looks to draw.
- New: **The tube's glass, over any look.** Chromatic shift pulls red and blue apart, and Curve, top & bottom and Curve, sides bow the picture like a tube; a tap still lands on what you see under your finger. Tilt, Bezel, Rounded corners, Vignette and Glass glare frame it. Old TV look sets all of it in one tap, and every slider has its own reset.
- New: **CRT presets.** Default, Arcade, Trinitron, Living room and VHS, then your own: Save keeps the look on screen, Export writes it to a file and Import brings one in. The presets and every CRT control are also at the end of Player Visuals Studio › Lyrics and in Trypt Mania's Graphics, so a look saved in one place is there in the others.
- New: **Lyrics on a curved tube.** Curve: top & bottom and Curve: sides, under 3D Letter Wave in Player Visuals Studio › Lyrics, bow the lyrics like an old CRT screen, the shadow and god rays with them. Curve chooses all the lyrics, the sung line or the sung word. Android 13 and later.

## Look

- Removed: **The old frosted glass on lists.** Song rows, library tiles, folders, cards, the mixer and the EQ are plain Material 3 surfaces now, with liquid glass on or off, instead of the dark frosted pane with its bright rim, and they always draw, even on the lowest-end phones. Where the panels' glass can't run, the nav bar is Android's own Material 3 bar.
- Changed: **Titles over descriptions.** A title is SemiBold and the line under it Regular, two weights apart, and titles are a size smaller. Titles that were bold or black (sheets, headers, Settings, Discover cards, the Theme Store) are lighter. No row changes height.
- Removed: **The Now Playing group in Settings.** Its rows repeated settings found elsewhere: View Mode is what a tap on the artwork changes, and the player's tint follows Appearance › Dynamic Colors. If you had turned Dynamic Player Color or Blurred Album Background off, they are on again.
- Fixed: **No grey band when the bar folds.** Scrolling a list folds the mini player into the nav bar and back, and each fold flashed a grey slab across the bottom of the screen. Now the bar going fades out before the one coming fades in.

## Fonts

- New: **A typography table.** Settings › Fonts has a table of every kind of text in the app, fifteen styles from big numbers to badges, each with its own font, weight from Thin to Black, size, width and letter spacing. Each style's name is drawn as the app draws it. A style you leave alone follows the main font, each has its own reset, and Reset all puts every one back.
- New: **Letter width.** The Width slider on the Fonts page makes every letter narrower or wider, from 70% to 130%, and a style in the table can have a width of its own. A font made with a width axis, such as IBM Plex Sans, draws its own narrow letters; any other is squeezed or stretched.
- Changed: **A condensed Fonts page.** The font you are trying is one row with its Use button. Under it one panel holds the text size, moved here from Appearance with Use system font size, and Width. The fonts are chips drawn in their own letters: hold an imported one to delete it, or tap + Import.
- Fixed: **Imported fonts in all their weights.** A font imported as one file per weight came in as a separate font for each file, and every weight was drawn from the one you picked. A family's files are now one font, each file drawn at its own weight and slant, in the app and in the lyrics, and deleting it deletes them all.

- New: **Your library as a galaxy.** Library galaxy, under Folders in Library, shows every artist on your phone as a star beside their genre on the genre map. Artists whose genre isn't recognised float above and below the disc.
- New: **Albums in orbit.** An artist's first album orbits nearest and each later one further out; albums you play a lot glow brighter. After eight albums the rest form an asteroid belt, and an album's extra tracks a ring. Tap a moon to play it, long-press a star to list every album and track.
- New: **Planets drawn from their sound.** An album's atmosphere is the colour of its key, a loud master hazes it wider, its cover turns at the album's tempo, and its night side is as deep as its dynamic range. A track's moon goes round at the track's tempo and glows in its key. Tempo, key and ReplayGain come from your tags, and a local track's dynamic range is measured the first time it plays through.
- New: **Works offline.** Everything on it comes from the music and play history on your phone.
- New: **Hold a song to hear it.** Press and hold a song in any list to hear up to 25 seconds of it; let go and it stops. It starts at the song's drop when Trypt Mania has found one, and the row fills from the left as it plays. The music already playing pauses and carries on when you let go, and a preview never reaches the queue or your scrobbles. Streams preview at 320 kbps so they start at once.
- Changed: **Select is in the song menu.** Holding a song now plays its preview, so choosing several songs starts from Select in a song's ⋮ menu. After that a tap adds or removes a song, as before.
- Changed: **Short tracks in your library.** A library scan no longer leaves out tracks under 30 seconds, so intros, interludes and skits are in your albums. With no folders picked, a short file comes in only beside its album's longer tracks, so voice notes and app sounds stay out; in the folders you pick, every length comes in.
- New: **Search and sort Downloads.** The Downloads page has the same sort toolbar and search bar as Liked Songs, with file size as a sort of its own. Tapping a song queues the list as shown.
- New: **Choose a song row's badges.** Settings › Appearance › Song row badges turns off the source (Local, Qobuz, TIDAL), quality, format, download and length marks on every song list, one by one or all at once, so a song's artist and album get the whole line.
- New: **Import a Spotify playlist from its link.** Create playlist opens on a link box: paste a Spotify playlist link (or tap Paste) and it shows the playlist's cover, name and length, fills in the name, and imports it. No exportify.net and no file. Any public playlist works without signing in, and Spotify then shares its first 100 songs; a longer one says so. With Spotify connected, your own playlists come in whole. Songs are matched several at a time, so a long playlist imports in a fraction of the time. Exported files are on the File tab, as before.
- New: **Exportify inside the app.** For what a link can't bring (a private playlist, Liked Songs, a playlist over 100 songs) the link box has Open Exportify. exportify.net opens inside the app: sign in with any Spotify account, Free included, tap Export beside a playlist or Export All, and it comes straight into your library, with no file to find. An import keeps going if you leave the screen. Connecting Spotify in the app works only for the few accounts Spotify has approved for it; Exportify signs in under its own registration, which is not held to that. Spotify's Google and Apple sign-in may not open inside an app; your Spotify email and password do.
- New: **Track info.** A song's menu (its ⋮) has Track info: its file's format, sample rate, bit depth, bitrate, length and size, and every tag in it by name, ReplayGain and MusicBrainz included, ready to select and copy. A streamed song shows its download or cached copy, or else what the app knows of it. The menu itself is glass now, over the list it came from. It slides up from above the bottom bar instead of covering it, and turns to Track info and back as a page.
- Fixed: **Recently played keeps your local songs.** A song tapped in the app, and a local song the player moved on to by itself, were never written to Recently played, so local listening hardly showed there. Every song is now recorded once it starts playing, whatever it plays from, and counted once (a repeat counts again). Pull down on the Library page to refresh the list.
- Fixed: **Track info for MP4 files.** A 5.1 AAC file showed "-14" channels and a bit depth, and ALAC showed as AAC. Format, channels, rate and depth now come from the file's own description of its audio: "AAC-LC" with "6 (5.1)", "ALAC" at its real depth, E-AC-3 marked when it carries Dolby Atmos.

## Now playing

- Changed: **Long titles show in full.** A song title too long for the player slides along to show the rest of itself, then back to its start, instead of ending in "…".
- Changed: **Glass Visualizer and Waveform buttons**, like Timer, Sound, Speed and Mixer above them, and a glass speed (or BPM) pill in the top bar.
- Changed: **The cover holds still.** The cover no longer punches in on each kick drum. Wave Candy's Kick punch swells the scope's lines instead, and the Punch strength slider is gone.
- Fixed: **The visualizer no longer flashes with vsync off.** With vsync disabled it flashed and disappeared between frames, the background visualizer showing the bare cover every other frame. Frames the frame-rate cap held back were still put on screen, empty; it now waits for the next frame instead, and never shows one it did not draw.
- Fixed: **Glass corners on the audio tools sheet.** Its glass edge stopped short of each corner and the corners looked cut off. The edge now runs all the way round, as on the pills; the mixer's channel strips get the same edge.
- Changed: **A glass pill picks the speed unit.** In the Speed panel, Multiplier, Semitones and BPM are marked by a pill of glass instead of a solid fill. It slides to the one you tap, and you can drag it freely along the bar; it settles on the nearest when you let go.
- Fixed: **BPM at the right tempo.** The Speed panel's BPM read hardcore and DnB at half and some slow songs at double, and stopped at 200. It now takes the fastest count whose beats nearly all hold a kick or a snare, however loud each one is in the mix, up to 250, and settles on what most of its readings agree on rather than the first. A song on your phone with a BPM tag shows its tag at once; a tap on the number still measures it from the audio. One known miss: a DnB two-step whose snare sits far under its kick can still read at half.
- Fixed: **Panels read in light themes.** With dynamic colour in a light theme, the Speed panel, the audio tools and the preset browser had dark text on dark glass and lost their outlines. Their text is light and their pills and switches show.

## Lyrics

- New: **Lyrics timed by ear.** A song with no word-timed lyrics is sent to the built-in chart server after fifteen seconds of play, so a song you skip is never sent. The server listens to the vocals and times every word, keeping the words the app already found. Every later play uses them. Settings › Appearance › Server lyrics turns this off.
- New: **Time lyrics on the server, by hand.** Time lyrics on the server is in the player's ⋮ menu and under an empty lyrics view. A song that isn't on your phone is downloaded first and kept. Several songs are timed at once, under a notification that names each song and its stage.
- New: **A lyrics editor.** Player ⋮ › Edit lyrics shows the lyrics as the player draws them, with the song's controls and Stamp now, which gives the selected line (or word, in Words mode) the current moment and moves to the next. Lines can be nudged, shifted together, edited, inserted, deleted or split into words, with undo and redo. Paste plain words or an LRC, Share as LRC, and Save; Forget saved lyrics goes back to looking them up.
- New: **Word-timed .lrc with your downloads.** With Settings › Downloads › Download Lyrics on, words timed by the server or saved in the editor are written beside the song's download as a word-timed .lrc.
- Fixed: **Songs named by number.** Untagged songs named like "2-05 Song" or "CD1 - 05 - Song" are searched without the number, and no longer get "01" or "CD1" as their artist. Refresh the library once to fix songs already scanned.
- Fixed: **Compilations.** Songs on a Various Artists album are searched under their own artist.
- Fixed: **The right lyrics for your own files.** Songs on your phone are no longer looked up on TIDAL by a made-up number, which could bring back another song's lyrics.

## Playback

- Fixed: **Seeking in ALAC files from Apple.** They are fragmented MP4 without an index, which the player treated as unseekable with no known length, so the seek bar would not move them. A file on your phone is now indexed when it opens, by its fragments' headers alone, and seeks like any other. An Apple Music stream is not: indexing one meant reading the whole stream before the first note, so it starts at once and plays through, as before.
- Changed: **Blends need gapless.** Blend between tracks only runs with Gapless playback on. A blend plays the outgoing song on a second player; with gapless off there is now only ever the one, and each song plays on its own from start to end. The blend slider is greyed out while gapless is off, and says why. Turning gapless off during a blend ends it.
- Changed: **No blends in exclusive mode.** With Exclusive USB DAC on, Blend between tracks is greyed out and says why, and no second player is made: a blend mixes two songs, and exclusive mode is the bit-perfect path. Songs play one at a time, gapless with Gapless playback on, and the blend you set comes back when exclusive mode is turned off. Turning exclusive mode on during a blend ends it.

## Equalizer

- New: **Your EQ on a Nothing Ear.** Export to Nothing Ear, the QR button beside Export EQ on the Precision AutoEQ screen, fits your EQ, bass and treble included, into the earbuds' eight bands as a QR code, and says how close the eight bands come. Scan it in Nothing X under Equalizer › Advanced, or tap Save image to scan it from another screen, and the earbuds play it without the app.
- Fixed: **The preamp turns bass and treble down too.** With the AutoEQ correction off, the Preamp was dropped, so a bass boost played at full level and the Preamp slider did nothing. It now applies whenever the correction or a tone knob changes the sound; with neither, the audio passes untouched, as before.

## Exclusive USB DAC

- Changed: **More DACs in exclusive mode.** DACs in USB Audio 3.0's basic mode describe no formats of their own, and exclusive mode used to give up on them. Apple's USB-C to 3.5 mm adapter is one, because Android always puts it in that mode. They now play at 48 kHz, in 24-bit.
- Changed: **DACs at their full output.** The DAC's own volume is unmuted and set to full, never past 0 dB, whenever a song starts, as USB Audio Player Pro does. Android's volume never reaches a DAC in exclusive mode, so a DAC left turned down stayed down. The app's volume is the only volume now, and still starts at −24 dB. The DAC card shows the result, as HW vol 0 dB.
- Fixed: **DACs that refused every song.** Some DACs were turned away with "no alt setting matches" whatever played. Those whose packets are sized exactly to their rate, Apple's adapter included, play now. A song at a rate the DAC can't take, such as 44.1 kHz on a basic-mode DAC, says so and plays through Android instead.
- Changed: **One volume bar.** The player no longer draws a DAC volume bar of its own. The volume keys bring up the DAC's bar at the top of the screen on every screen, the player included, and it hides again 2.5 seconds after the last press.
- Fixed: **A full-volume burst between songs.** A song change could play a moment of noise at the DAC's full volume, whatever the volume was set to, with gapless on or off. Exclusive mode sets the DAC to 0 dB and turns the volume down in software, and the samples were limited only after that: a sample far past full scale, such as a decoder's 16-bit samples read as float, came out at full scale. Every sample is now limited before the volume. The player that plays the outgoing song during a crossfade also decodes as the main one does now, with the fix for Samsung's FLAC decoder (16-bit files reported as float) it was missing.
- Changed: **The DAC card shows the DAC's release**, as rev, where it used to be labelled as the USB version.
- Removed: **USB DAC bit-perfect routing.** It pointed Android's output at a USB DAC, still through Android's own mixer. Exclusive USB DAC is the bit-perfect path, so it is the one switch now; the first-run setup says where to find it.

## Account and stats

- New: **Your own picture and name.** The account button in the top bar is glass: your picture (or initials) when you're signed in, a Sign in pill when you're not. The Account page picks a picture from your photos, or goes back to your Google one, and you choose a username of your own instead of showing your Google name.
- New: **Listening stats, rebuilt.** Account › Listening stats is a column of glass cards: time listened against the period before, your listening type from an hour-by-weekday grid, top artist, on repeat, discovery, a streak calendar and audio quality. Dig deeper opens fourteen charts of sessions, months, variety, track lengths, sources, weekdays, albums and tracks; tap a bar and it says what it holds.
- New: **Achievements.** Ten goals beside your stats, from 25 new artists in 30 days and a 30-day streak to trying every settings page and every plugin. Tap one to see what is left to do and a button that takes you there. They stay on your phone, and clearing settings keeps them.
